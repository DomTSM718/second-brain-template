## Overview

Weekly snapshots of the brain. `/learn` appends to the current week as work happens;
`/grow` diffs the latest week against the previous one to report growth.

**One section per week, newest last.** Never rewrite an earlier week - the whole point
is the trend line. If a week was quiet, say so and move on; a gap in the log reads as
lost data rather than as a quiet week.
